## Key Features for Wedding, Portrait & Event Photographers
1. **GroupLab (Group Photo Row & Safe Aperture Calculator):** Solves the #1 wedding stress moment ("60 guests waiting in the sun, 5m space behind the photographer, 35mm lens ‚Äî which f-stop do I need so Row 1 to Row 4 are all pin-sharp?"). Calculates optimal staggered row staging (Row 1 seated/kneeling, Row 2 standing, Row 3+ elevated), exact focus point (**Row 2 Rule** for 1/3 front and 2/3 rear depth of field), required camera distance, exact minimum safe f-stop (`f/4`, `f/5.6`, `f/8`, `f/11`) for Full Frame, APS-C, and Micro Four Thirds sensors, plus wide-angle edge distortion warnings below 35mm.
2. **FlickerGuard (50 Hz / 60 Hz Anti-Flicker & LED Safe-Shutter Guide):** Prevents dark horizontal PWM LED banding stripes when shooting with silent electronic shutter in churches, wedding venues, theaters, or in front of keynote LED video walls and projectors. Classifies all 88 cameras by sensor readout speed tier (Tier 1 Global Shutter / Stacked CMOS like Sony A9 III, A1 II, Nikon Z8/Z9, Canon R1/R3/R5 II vs. Standard Rolling CMOS like A7 IV, R6 II, Z6 II) and calculates physically safe shutter harmonics (`1/50 s`, `1/100 s` for 50 Hz; `1/60 s`, `1/125 s` for 60 Hz) plus verified camera menu paths for Anti-Flicker Shoot and High-Frequency Variable Shutter (Synchro Scan / HF Anti-Flicker).
3. **Confetti Lock & 14 Wedding, Stage & Portrait Scenarios:** Translates 14 critical on-set moments into exact AF tracking sensitivity (e.g., locking AF onto the bride & groom during confetti/rice exits so focus never jumps onto flying petals in front of their faces), exposure targets, and EFCS bokeh clipping warnings (`> 1/2000 s` with `f/1.2` and `f/1.4` portrait primes).
4. **EventKeys (Wedding & Event Custom Button Cheat-Sheet):** Stores personal custom button mappings (`AF-ON`, `AEL`, `C1‚ÄìC4`, `M-Fn`, `Fn1/Fn2`, Control Ring) offline for instant **Left/Right Eye AF Switch**, **Silent vs. Mechanical Shutter Toggle**, **Registered Face Priority**, and **Confetti AF Lock**.
5. **3 Venue Display Modes (including Church Stealth Mode):** Switch in 1 tap between **Studio Dark Mode** (Warm Espresso Obsidian), **Sunlight Mode** (high-contrast Warm Ivory for bright outdoor champagne receptions), and **Church Stealth Mode** (pure OLED black `#000000` with warm candlelight amber typography and 0% blue light so guests in dark church pews or concert halls are never disturbed).
6. **100% Offline & Fair One-Time Pricing:** Works completely without Wi-Fi or cell reception inside thick-walled churches, historic castles, and underground event halls. Core Set-Guide is free; **Portraro Pro** is a **‚Ç¨1.99 / $1.99 one-time purchase** (`app.portraro.prof` / `app.portraro.pro`) ‚Äî no subscription, no ads, zero tracking.
